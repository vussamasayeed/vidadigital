# Vida Digital (EU)

Vida Digital is an Android marketplace app for hiring trusted local people across the EU for short, practical tasks under temporary contracts.

## What is included
- Kotlin + Jetpack Compose Android app scaffold
- Branded home screen focused on task marketplace flows
- Product brief notes for provider operations, authorization, and logistics

## Core concept
Vida Digital helps users:
1. Post tasks that need a local person in another city/country.
2. Hire verified providers with clear temporary authorization.
3. Track completion with proof, updates, and delivery status.

## Example tasks
- Wait in line at an office or public service center
- Provide translator support for visits or calls
- Go to an office and gather specific information/documents
- Collect forgotten items from hotels and ship them internationally

## Example scenario
You travel back to Barcelona and realize your watch was left at a hotel in Poland.
With Vida Digital, you can:
- Post a retrieval request.
- Hire a verified provider near the hotel.
- Authorize pickup via hotel phone/email confirmation.
- Receive the watch by shipment to Barcelona.
