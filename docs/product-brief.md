# Vida Digital EU — Product Brief

## Vision
Build an EU-first mobile marketplace where customers can instantly hire local providers for practical tasks (line waiting, translation, office visits, pickups) with secure temporary contracts.

## Key problem
People frequently need trusted, on-the-ground help in another city/country for one-time tasks they cannot do themselves.

## Core task categories
1. **Line waiting** — provider queues at government or service offices on behalf of customer
2. **Translation support** — provider helps with language barriers by call or in person
3. **Office information gathering** — provider visits offices to collect requested information/documents
4. **Item retrieval and forwarding** — provider collects forgotten belongings and ships them

## Example use case
- Customer leaves a watch at a hotel in Poland and travels back to Barcelona.
- Hotel stores the watch at reception.
- Customer hires a verified Vida Digital provider in Poland.
- Customer authorizes the provider via hotel-approved phone call or email.
- Provider picks up the watch, ships it to Barcelona, and uploads proof.

## MVP features
1. Task request creation with category, location, SLA, and budget
2. Provider marketplace with verification badges, ratings, and pricing
3. Temporary contract generation with identity + authorization fields
4. Authorization toolkit (email template + call verification checklist)
5. In-app messaging and task milestone tracking
6. Proof upload (photo, receipt, office ticket, shipment tracking)
7. Payment escrow and release on successful completion

## Compliance and trust
- GDPR-compliant user/provider data handling
- Identity verification and fraud checks for providers
- Audit trail for authorization, calls, and task milestones
- Dispute process with evidence review and refund logic

## KPIs
- Task posted to provider acceptance rate
- Task completion rate by category
- Median completion time
- Dispute rate and resolution time
- Repeat usage within 30 days
